Fabric Care Guide

1. Cotton
Washing: Machine wash in cold or warm water, preferably below 86°F (30°C). Use a mild detergent and separate light and dark colors.
Drying: Air drying is recommended to reduce shrinkage. If using a dryer, choose low or medium heat.
Care: Cotton may wrinkle after washing. Iron at medium heat when necessary.

2. Denim
Washing: Turn jeans inside out before washing. Use cold water and a mild detergent to reduce fading.
Drying: Air dry when possible. Avoid frequent high-heat drying because it may shrink the fabric.
Care: Denim does not need to be washed after every wear. Spot cleaning can help extend garment life.

3. Wool and Cashmere
Washing: Dry cleaning is recommended for structured garments. Hand wash suitable items in cold water with wool-safe detergent.
Drying: Lay flat to dry. Do not hang wet wool because the garment may stretch.
Care: Fold sweaters for storage and use breathable garment bags for long-term storage.

4. Silk
Washing: Check the care label first. Dry cleaning is recommended for many silk garments. Hand-washable silk should be cleaned gently in cold water.
Drying: Do not wring. Air dry away from direct sunlight.
Care: Use low heat or steam when removing wrinkles.

5. Polyester
Washing: Machine wash in cool or warm water using a normal or gentle cycle.
Drying: Tumble dry on low heat or air dry.
Care: Polyester is resistant to wrinkles but can be damaged by excessive heat.

6. Linen
Washing: Machine wash or hand wash in cool water with mild detergent.
Drying: Air drying is preferred. Remove the garment while slightly damp if ironing is needed.
Care: Wrinkling is a natural characteristic of linen.

7. Down Jackets
Washing: Follow the care label. Use a down-specific detergent when machine washing is allowed.
Drying: Dry completely on low heat. Dryer balls can help restore loft.
Care: Store loosely in a dry place. Avoid long-term compression.
