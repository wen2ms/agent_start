Seasonal Wardrobe Guide

1. Spring
Recommended items: lightweight jackets, cotton shirts, chinos, jeans, cardigans, and light sweaters.
Recommended fabrics: cotton, lightweight denim, linen blends, and thin knitwear.
Layering is useful because temperatures may change significantly during the day.

2. Summer
Recommended items: T-shirts, short-sleeve shirts, shorts, lightweight dresses, skirts, and breathable trousers.
Recommended fabrics: cotton, linen, rayon blends, and lightweight performance fabrics.
Choose breathable clothing and lighter colors in hot weather.

3. Fall
Recommended items: denim jackets, overshirts, sweaters, hoodies, chinos, jeans, and light coats.
Recommended fabrics: denim, wool blends, corduroy, fleece, and thicker cotton.
Layering a T-shirt, sweater, and jacket makes it easier to adjust to changing temperatures.

4. Winter
Recommended items: insulated coats, wool coats, sweaters, thermal base layers, scarves, gloves, and boots.
Recommended fabrics: wool, fleece, down, synthetic insulation, and thermal fabrics.
For cold weather, layering usually works better than relying on one extremely thick garment.

5. Rainy Weather
Use a waterproof or water-resistant outer layer.
Water-resistant shoes or boots are useful during prolonged rain.
Allow wet clothing to dry completely before storage to prevent odor and mildew.

6. Travel Wardrobe
Choose neutral colors and items that can be combined in multiple outfits.
Pack layers rather than several bulky garments when possible.
Comfortable walking shoes are important for trips involving long periods of walking.
