Clothing Size Guide

1. General Women's Size Guide
Height: 5'0"-5'4", Weight: 90-110 lb, Recommended size: XS.
Height: 5'2"-5'6", Weight: 105-125 lb, Recommended size: S.
Height: 5'4"-5'8", Weight: 120-145 lb, Recommended size: M.
Height: 5'6"-5'10", Weight: 140-165 lb, Recommended size: L.
Height: 5'8"-6'0", Weight: 160-190 lb, Recommended size: XL.
Height: 5'10" and above, Weight: 185-220 lb, Recommended size: XXL.

2. General Men's Size Guide
Height: 5'4"-5'7", Weight: 110-135 lb, Recommended size: S.
Height: 5'6"-5'9", Weight: 130-155 lb, Recommended size: M.
Height: 5'8"-6'0", Weight: 150-180 lb, Recommended size: L.
Height: 5'10"-6'2", Weight: 175-205 lb, Recommended size: XL.
Height: 6'0"-6'4", Weight: 200-235 lb, Recommended size: XXL.
Height: 6'2" and above, Weight: 230 lb and above, Recommended size: 3XL.

3. Fit Notes
Sizing varies by brand and garment style.
Choose one size larger for an oversized or relaxed fit.
Choose the regular size for a standard fit.
For fitted jackets, trousers, or formalwear, chest, waist, hip, and inseam measurements are more reliable than height and weight alone.
Always check the brand-specific size chart before purchasing.
